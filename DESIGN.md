## Overview

Slides use Cedarville blue and gold on white, with serif headings and clear sans-serif body text. Illustrations lead visual explanations. Controls stay quieter than the presentation.

## Colors

Blue carries text and controls. Gold marks selected options and primary actions. Secondary text and thin dividers support the hierarchy. Gold is an accent rather than a body-text color.

## Typography

Headings use Minion Pro Display and body copy uses Myriad Pro, loaded from the University Adobe Fonts kit https://use.typekit.net/apf8ssc.css. The title uses a larger display scale; slide headings stay consistent. Titles carry the topic without extra labels above them.

## Layout

Use open columns and deliberate whitespace. Align related content directly, without surrounding cards. Images appear side by side for comparison. At widths up to 800px, columns stack and text remains readable. Desktop slides fit the projector viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat surfaces. No decorative shadows, gradients, or floating containers.

## Shapes

Use straight image edges and simple rules. Controls are text with an underline for emphasis or selection.

## Components

Navigation includes Previous, Next, a slide menu, and a count. Presenter view opens separate notes and synchronized navigation. Scenario selectors use a gold underline for the active item. Next cycles all three scenarios on slide 3 before advancing; manual tab selection updates the same navigation state. The cover QR sits below the mountain image, never over it. Next reveals each idea before advancing to another slide; Previous reverses the builds. Short fades guide focus without automatic progression. On the skills-stacking slide, the student illustration spotlights Miller Bot, Erasmus Bot, and Aristotle Bot before revealing the whole stack. Respect reduced-motion settings and print all content. The outside plugins slide uses four open entries with a creator and a concrete use for each, followed by a questions slide with speech bubbles. The final slide pairs one closing thought with a large share QR code; navigation chrome is hidden. Reference links remain in presenter notes and the printed final slide. The timeline keeps a plain-text source attribution. QR codes combine a full-size tonal backpack with Floyd-Steinberg halftoning, then restore QR module centers and function patterns following QRFrame’s Halftone technique. Keep navy and white contrast, H error correction, and a four-module quiet zone. Decode actual rendered sizes before publishing.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do preserve the slide order, content, audience activities, and presenter controls.
- Do use the existing generated illustrations for the cul-de-sac and traveling backpack.
- Do keep source links in notes, with the full reference list in the closing notes.
- Do keep all revealed text at full contrast, especially tables and audience activities.
- Do use spoken prompts for audience participation, without projected checkboxes or website calls to action.
- Do hide the header tools in full screen, while keeping Next and Previous available.
- Don't introduce repeated rounded cards, colored callout boxes, badges, or labels above headings.
- Don't add disclaimers to slides or presenter notes.
